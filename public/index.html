🔌 Connect your Moomoo account

Your Moomoo login never touches this website. A program called OpenD runs on your own computer and holds the login; a small read-only helper sits next to it and your browser talks to the helper.

  1. Install Moomoo OpenD from Moomoo's OpenAPI page and log in with your Moomoo account in the OpenD window.
  2. Install Python, then run pip install moomoo-api.
  3. Get this project's files on that computer, then run python3 bridge/check_setup.py. It tells you what is missing.
  4. Start the helper with a private token and this site's address:
    BRIDGE_TOKEN=your-long-random-text BRIDGE_ALLOW_ORIGIN=https://your-site python3 bridge/opend_bridge.py
    Add BRIDGE_ALLOW_REAL=1 if you want to read your real account.
  5. Use Chrome or Firefox (Safari blocks this), open 💼 Portfolio → 🔄 Sync with my Moomoo account, enter the token and press Import.

Read-only: nothing here can place an order. Never type your Moomoo password, trade password or API keys into this page or any chat. The full guide is in docs/connect-moomoo.md.

📖 Glossary: plain-English meanings
Stop (stop-loss)
A price you decide in advance to sell at if the stock keeps falling. It limits a small loss from becoming a big one.
Trailing stop
A stop that moves up as the stock rises, so you keep more of your profit. It never moves down.
Target
The price where you would start taking profit. Here it is 20% above what you paid.
Slot
One of your 3 positions. Fewer, better-watched stocks beat many you cannot follow.
Cash buffer
Spare money you do not invest (RM200 here), so you are never forced to sell at a bad time.
Ex-dividend
The cut-off day for a dividend. The share price usually drops by about the dividend that day, which is normal.
Daily swing
How much a stock normally moves in a day. A stop tighter than that is likely to trigger by accident.
Reality check
How often this stock's own past year would have hit your stop before your target. A guide, not a forecast.
Delayed quote
A price about 15 minutes old. Fine for planning, not for split-second decisions.
Paper account
A practice account with pretend money.
🧠 How the Today page decides
  • Do now comes from your own rules: a stop was hit, a stock is up 30% (trim half), you are down 10% with no written plan, spare cash is under RM200, you hold more than 3 stocks, saved prices are old, or there is bad news on a stock you own.
  • Ideas worth a look are stocks on your watchlist (that you do not own) with positive news in the last two weeks and no bad news in the last 7 days. Newer news counts more. It reads headlines only, so it says nothing about price or value.
  • Good to know covers ex-dividend days in the next 10 days, fresh results, analyst upgrades on your stocks, and stocks to avoid for now.
  • Prices come from Yahoo Finance (about 15 minutes delayed) or from your Moomoo account if you sync. A price you typed more recently is never overwritten.
  • Your rules: stop 8% below what you paid, move it to break-even at +10%, lock in +10% at +20%, trim half at +30%, max 3 stocks, risk at most 2% of your money per trade.

ℹ️ About & important notes
  • Educational only. Not financial advice. This tool reads public information and does arithmetic. It cannot see the future, and you can lose money.
